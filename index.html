🌳 Family tree of species

Each band is a species, from when it appeared to now (or until it died out, ▏); its thickness is its population (a gap means it died out and migrants brought it back). Branches show where a species split off its parent. Shaded stripes are random events. Hover a species to see its relatives and DNA; click a living one to follow it.
